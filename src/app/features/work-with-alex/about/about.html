About Me

I build maintainable, performant Angular applications and lead teams to deliver product-focused solutions.

I've spent the last nine years diving deep into Angular, where I've found my passion for creating user interfaces that aren't just visually appealing but also easy to use and efficient for developers.

Throughout my career, I've worked on a range of projects for well-known clients like Porsche, Daimler, Emil Frey, KUKA, DVAG, TradingPoint (XM), and Atruvia — while pursuing my studies in the German line of the Computer Science Faculty at Babeș-Bolyai University.

While I'm comfortable working independently, I've come to appreciate the power of teamwork. Now, I'm focused on creating a collaborative atmosphere where everyone can succeed and push boundaries together.
